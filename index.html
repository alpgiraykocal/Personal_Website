The experiments below came out of that. They are personal learning projects, not products, and they are live if you want to look around.

Three things I built and keep running. None of them replaces your own compliance process — treat whatever they surface as a lead to verify.

Sanctions Search

Screens names, entities and vessels against OFAC, EU, UN and UK sanctions lists plus US export-control data. Fuzzy multi-script matching, ID and vessel lookups, and 50%-Rule ownership network views. Daily refresh, nothing stored.

Compliance Check-Up

An AML/CFT self-assessment: a 218-question survey, a risk-based QA sampling plan and action tracking, with a dashboard over the results. No install, no account — data stays in your browser.

Blockchain Analysis

Reads Bitcoin, Ethereum and TRON as a graph: transaction flow, co-spend clustering into entities, and attribution-driven risk. Its investigation workspace tests activity against named laundering typologies, and every finding also states the ordinary explanation that fits it. Live public explorers only — no keys, no accounts, no database.
